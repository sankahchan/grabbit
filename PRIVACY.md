# Privacy Policy — Grabbit Web Grabber

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

## What this extension does

Grabbit Web Grabber detects videos and downloadable files on web pages you visit and sends them to the Grabbit download manager app on your Mac.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to remote servers.**

When you click "Download with Grabbit":
- The file URL and page URL are sent **locally** to the Grabbit app on your Mac via Chrome's native messaging API.
- Cookies may be included so the app can download files that require login.
- No data leaves your computer. No analytics. No tracking.

## Permissions explained

- **activeTab, scripting:** Detect video elements on the current page.
- **contextMenus:** Add "Download with Grabbit" to right-click menus.
- **nativeMessaging:** Communicate with the local Grabbit app.
- **cookies:** Include login cookies for protected downloads (only when you initiate a download).
- **webRequest:** Observe network requests to find direct media URLs.
- **storage:** Save your extension preferences locally.
- **downloads, notifications:** Fallback download and user notifications.
- **Host access (`<all_urls>`):** Detect media on any site you visit. The extension only acts when you click it.
